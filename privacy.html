Privacy Policy

Tuner for iPhone and iPad. Effective 8 November 2026.

The short version

Tuner collects nothing. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • The playlist address you add. It is kept in the iOS keychain on this device only, because addresses often contain a username and password. It is not backed up to other devices.
  • Favourites, recently watched channels and settings. These hold channel names and numbers, never stream addresses or passwords.
  • Diagnostic logs. Tuner writes a log of what the player is doing so problems can be understood. Usernames, passwords and tokens are blanked out before anything is written. The logs stay in the app's folder, which you can open in the Files app; they leave your device only if you choose to share them.

Who Tuner connects to

Only the playlist address you add, and the addresses that playlist lists: its channel streams, programme guide and channel logos. If you try the built-in sample channels, Tuner connects to the public test servers they come from. As with any connection, those servers see your device's IP address; their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it. We do not run or control them.

Third parties

Video playback uses VLCKit, open-source software from VideoLAN that runs entirely on your device. Tuner includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

Children

Tuner is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

"Change source" in the app removes the saved playlist address. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
