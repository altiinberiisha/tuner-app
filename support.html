Tuner Support

Tuner plays live TV from an M3U playlist you add. It comes with no channels, playlists or subscriptions of its own, and does not sell or recommend any.

Adding a playlist

Paste your playlist's address into Playlist address and tap Connect. If your TV service gave you a link of the form http://server:port/get.php?username=…&password=…, paste that whole link: Tuner then also shows the programme guide and your account's expiry and connections.

No playlist yet? Try the sample channels plays openly licensed test streams, so you can see how the app works.

A channel will not play

  • "Your provider says the account is busy": many services allow one stream at a time. Stop playback on your other devices; Tuner retries by itself and plays as soon as the service lets go, usually within a few minutes.
  • "No signal": the stream stopped or never started. Tap Try again, or try another channel. If no channel plays, check the playlist still works with your service.
  • "Couldn't load your channels": Tuner could not reach the playlist address. Check your connection and tap Try again.

4K on mobile data

When a channel turns out to be real 4K, by the picture that arrives rather than its name, Tuner asks before playing it on mobile data, since 4K uses a lot of data. You can turn this off in the menu at the top right.

Picture in picture

Tap the picture-in-picture button in the player, or leave the app while a channel plays, and the picture keeps playing in a small window over other apps.

Sending a log

If something goes wrong, the log helps. In the Files app, go to On My iPhone › Tuner › Logs and share tuner.log and vlc.log with your message. Usernames and passwords are blanked out of the logs.
